Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

Welcome to Aulert. Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and what controls you have, with a specific focus on our integration with Google APIs.

1. Information We Access

Aulert operates primarily as a client-side web application. Your academic data is fetched directly from Google's servers to your local browser. We do not operate traditional backend data-collection servers for the core service. To provide its features, Aulert requests secure, scoped access to:

  • Google Classroom Data: Your enrolled courses, announcements, coursework (assignments), submission status, and grades.
  • Google Profile Data: Your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) to personalize your dashboard.
  • Google Calendar Data (Optional): If you enable calendar sync, we access your primary Google Calendar to create and manage deadline events. This is an opt-in feature.
  • Gmail Metadata (Optional): If private-comment alerts are enabled, Aulert reads the relevant Google Classroom email metadata in your browser to surface those alerts. It does not use Gmail to send the discontinued daily digest.
  • Discord Notifications (Optional): If you connect Discord, we request offline Google Classroom access so our server can check for new Classroom items and post them to the Discord webhooks you select.

2. How We Use Google Data

Aulert's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use your data strictly to provide the core functionality of Aulert: displaying a unified dashboard of your classes and deadlines.
  • Your data is fetched directly from Google's servers to your local browser. We use essential browser storage for preferences and the short-lived session; optional analytics is loaded only after you accept it in the cookie banner. We use Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand page views and performance, helping us improve Aulert without tracking personally identifiable information or using tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google data. If you enable Discord Notifications, the selected Classroom updates are sent to the Discord destinations you configure; those destinations are third-party services governed by their own terms and privacy practices.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core service.
  • We do not allow humans to read your data, except where necessary to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit prior consent for support purposes.

3. Discord Notifications (Optional Backend Feature)

If you opt in to Discord Notifications, the following additional data handling applies:

  • What is stored: An encrypted Google OAuth refresh token and encrypted Discord webhook URLs are stored on our backend (Vercel serverless functions + Firebase Realtime Database). We keep delivery timestamps and bounded de-duplication identifiers, not a remote copy of your Classroom feed.
  • Purpose: The refresh token is used only to read new Classroom announcements, assignments, and materials. Aulert checks about every 10 minutes and posts concise alerts to your selected Discord destinations.
  • Security: Tokens and webhook URLs are encrypted before storage. Communication with Google and Discord uses HTTPS, and the scheduled endpoint is protected by a secret bearer token. Webhook URLs are not returned to the browser after setup.
  • How to opt out: You can disable this feature at any time from the Settings tab in Aulert. Disconnecting it immediately and permanently deletes the stored webhook URLs and offline authorization from our servers.
  • Token revocation: If your refresh token becomes invalid (e.g., you revoke access from Google), our system automatically detects this and removes your stale data entry.

4. Cross-Account Protection

Aulert participates in Google's Risk and Incident Sharing and Coordination (RISC) program. If Google detects a major security event regarding your account (e.g., your account is compromised, disabled, or its tokens are revoked), Google may send us a secure notification (a Security Event Token). We use this information solely to immediately revoke your active session and force a logout to protect your local data. We do not store or share RISC event data beyond what is necessary for this protective action.

5. Data Retention

For the core service, Aulert does not retain your Classroom feed on remote servers. Essential browser storage can be cleared in your browser; analytics consent can be changed by clearing site data. For optional Discord Notifications, encrypted credentials and delivery records are retained only while the feature is enabled and are deleted immediately upon disconnection.

6. Revoking Access

You maintain complete control over your data. You can disconnect Aulert and revoke all access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security settings. Doing so will immediately prevent Aulert from accessing any of your Google data and will invalidate any stored refresh tokens.

7. Global Privacy Regulations (GDPR & CCPA)

Aulert is designed with privacy-first principles. We process data predominantly on the client side. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or California, you are granted specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the rights to access, delete, and restrict the processing of your data. Because Aulert stores your core data locally on your device, you can exercise these rights instantly by clearing your browser's local storage and revoking our Google API access. For optional backend features like Discord Notifications, disconnecting the feature immediately serves as your right to be forgotten by permanently deleting its stored authorization and webhooks. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Aulert is designed for students using Google Classroom. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the consent of a parent or school administrator acting under COPPA or equivalent regulations. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
